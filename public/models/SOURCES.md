Horse: the file is unmodified. Materials are replaced at runtime so the horse matches the other pieces:
body, mane and muzzle take the side's ivory/ebony lacquer, hooves take gold, eyes take ebony.
The horned-helm lancer riding it is our own geometry (`src/scene/rider.ts`), not from any asset.
`src/scene/rider.ts` is original code written for this project; it uses no external model, texture or other asset.
It stands on the knight's procedural turned base (foot + gold rings from `src/scene/pieces.ts`).

The earlier Quaternius "Animated Knight Pack" (human knight, step 4b v1) was removed in favour of the horse.

Pawn: the file is unmodified (no re-export). At runtime (`src/scene/figure.ts`):
- materials are replaced: armour, cloth, skin, leather, steel take the side's ivory/ebony lacquer,
  GOLD stays gold, EYES and SHADOW take ebony (the embedded texture is loaded but unused);
- the idle clip holds the arms out in a T-pose, so a lowered-arm rest pose is layered onto every clip
  (the spear stays upright in the right hand, and tips forward for the thrust);
- 16 zero-length normals in the cloth are pointed up (they rendered as NaN and blacked out the bloomed frame);
- it is scaled to 0.5 high with its boot soles (y ≈ 0 in the file; the armour alone ends 0.066 up)
  on a low procedural pedestal from `src/scene/pieces.ts`.

Rook: compressed from the user's Meshy export (master `Meshy_AI_rook_white_35k_1102142933_texture.glb`,
33,890,036 bytes, SHA-256 `22a78a62ae08dc87d5d33b2cf688b9e45e807b20945da54824da9493a45c3f3e`; the master
is kept outside the repo and must never be committed). Processing (glTF-Transform): textures resized to 2048 and
re-encoded as WebP, meshopt geometry compression + quantization, geometry unchanged (35,748 tris); the
`socket_weapon` node was added at the hammer grip. At runtime (`src/scene/statue.ts`) it is scaled to stand on
the board; the black rook is the same file with an obsidian recolour of the material and a crimson glow
(white keeps its texture with a gold glow). It is animated as one rigid object (no bones).

Pawn: compressed from the user's Meshy export (master `Meshy_AI_Pawn_Knight_Remeshed__1102152248_texture.glb`,
32,570,444 bytes, SHA-256 `583729926487a864fa7e35b5f1c5e6f86ae60ea6921b5868d177fab8950061b8`; kept outside the
repo, never committed). Same processing as the rook (dedup, prune, textures 2048 WebP q85, meshopt medium,
geometry unchanged at 35,633 tris; `socket_weapon` added at the spear grip). It replaces the spearman
`pawn/Pawn.glb` in the game through the same statue layer as the rook (same side finishes); `pawn/Pawn.glb`
stays in the repo only for `src/scene/figure.ts` and its test.

(dedup, prune, textures 2048 WebP q85, meshopt medium, geometry unchanged; `socket_weapon` added as a direct scene
child at the weapon grip, measured from the vertices). The masters are kept outside the repo and must never be
committed; they were received as `<piece>_white_master.glb` (the original Meshy export names were not kept):

- queen: master `queen_white_master.glb`, 29,646,676 bytes, SHA-256 `bceec54e1ecd1e3976dc0141518858bef00b5febf6b63d358723a71a67975f3f`; shipped 1,655,240 bytes, 35,748 tris
- bishop: master `bishop_white_master.glb`, 32,547,148 bytes, SHA-256 `cc0e5e1bbb9b34c2bb87acd2e27f9688075fdb09f46be5d8affcff60d32e5b36`; shipped 1,942,192 bytes, 35,320 tris
- knight: master `knight_white_master.glb`, 32,171,280 bytes, SHA-256 `1c75bdadad739ccb7dbe78fc0a24fab1f072d622454bc0a9aa94b64bc1c94cd7`; shipped 1,893,928 bytes, 35,935 tris

They stand on the board through the same statue layer as the rook and pawn (`src/scene/statue.ts`: scaled to the

Quaternius horse + rider above is loaded as the fallback instead.
