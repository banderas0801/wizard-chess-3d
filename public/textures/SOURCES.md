# Texture sources

All textures are 1k JPGs from Poly Haven, downloaded 2026-10-31 and unmodified. License: CC0 1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license).

| Files | Asset | Author | Source URL | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `monastery_stone_floor_{diff,nor_gl,rough}_1k.jpg` | Monastery Stone Floor | Amal Kumar | https://polyhaven.com/a/monastery_stone_floor | hall floor |
| `large_sandstone_blocks_01_{diff,nor_gl,rough}_1k.jpg` | Large Sandstone Blocks 01 | Rob Tuytel | https://polyhaven.com/a/large_sandstone_blocks_01 | columns and back wall |

Download pattern: `https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/<asset>/<asset>_<map>_1k.jpg`.
Board, piece and gold surfaces are procedural (generated in `src/scene/materials.ts`); no texture files.
